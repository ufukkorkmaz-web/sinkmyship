How to play

  1. Type your name and the room code, then place your 5 ships. Press Ready!
  2. Take turns. Click a square in Enemy waters to fire.
  3. Red peg = hit. White peg = miss.
  4. When every square of a ship is hit, it sinks.
  5. Sink all 5 enemy ships to win!
